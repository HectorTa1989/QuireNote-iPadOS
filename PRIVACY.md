# Quire Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-11-10**

Quire is designed so your notebooks remain files you control. This policy applies
to the Quire iPad app.

## Data Quire does not collect

Quire does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your notebook contents,
handwriting, imported PDFs, recognized handwriting text, notebook filenames, or
search terms. Handwriting recognition occurs on the device. Quire has no user
accounts and operates no Quire cloud service.

## Where your notes live

You choose the folder where Quire stores notebook packages. That folder may be on
your iPad or in a third-party file provider such as iCloud Drive. The provider you
choose governs any syncing, backup, retention, or sharing of that folder under its
own privacy terms. Quire accesses the folder only after you select it in the
system file picker.

## Purchases

Quire Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase. Purchases and restorations are
processed by Apple through StoreKit. Quire uses the resulting Apple entitlement
only to determine whether Pro is unlocked; it does not receive payment-card data.

## Changes and contact

Material changes will be published in this file before taking effect. For privacy
questions, open an issue at <https://github.com/HectorTa1989/Quire-iPadOS/issues>.
