## Description

QuireNote is a calm, files-first notebook for iPad and Apple Pencil.

Write naturally on beautiful paper templates, import PDFs, and search your
handwriting on-device. Your notebooks stay in the folder you choose, with an
editable notebook package and a portable PDF copy.

Why QuireNote:

- Fast, natural Apple Pencil writing
- Lined, blank, grid, and dot paper
- Import a PDF and write directly on it
- On-device handwriting recognition and search
- One-tap undo and reliable autosave
- Files you control in iCloud Drive or On My iPad
- No account and no subscription

QuireNote Pro unlocks unlimited notebooks with one purchase. Your notes remain
your files, even if you open them outside QuireNote.

## App Review notes

QuireNote stores notebooks in a user-selected folder and performs handwriting
recognition on-device. The app does not require an account. To test the core
workflow: choose a folder, create a notebook, write on a page, close and reopen
it, then use search. PDF import is available from the Add menu after a folder
has been selected.
